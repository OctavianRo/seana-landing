How it works

Four steps to
your first booking.

Submitted locations and verification requests stay linked to your account, so you can pick up where you left off.

  1. 01 / YOUR OWNER ACCOUNT

    Sign in with your business email

    Create your account in the business portal. Your email is verified as you go.

  2. 02 / YOUR SAUNA

    Claim a listing or add a location

    Search the directory for your sauna, or add one we don’t have yet — both from your dashboard.

  3. 03 / YOUR DETAILS, VERIFIED

    We confirm you’re authorised

    Our team checks the sauna is real and that you can act for it. Nothing goes public before that.

  4. 04 / READY FOR BOOKINGS

    Set prices and connect payments

    Finish the setup checklist and connect Stripe, then launch when you’re ready.

Is seána live yet?＋

The app is launching soon. We’re preparing owner setup so you can claim your listing, verify ownership and get ready for bookings.

What does it cost?＋

Get in touch to discuss pricing and payment fees for your business. We’ll explain the details before you commit.

Can I join with more than one location?＋

Yes. Use the same owner account to claim or add each location. Each sauna has its own details, verification and payment setup.

What if my sauna isn’t open yet?＋

We’d still love to hear from you. Contact us with your expected opening date and we’ll talk about getting ready for launch.
